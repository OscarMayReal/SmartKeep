# SmartKeep

SmartKeep is a application for managing collections of assets. It lets you keep track of where your assets are and who has them. It is built with Next.js and TypeScript. Don't fall into the trap of losing things. Use SmartKeep to keep track of where your assets are and who has them.

## Features

- creating, deleting, and updating assets
- creating locations
- checking in and checking out assets
